## English full description

IMPOSSIBLE LEVELS is a fast, clever puzzle game built around one question: can you solve what looks impossible?

Each level is short, readable, and designed around a surprising interaction. Tap, drag, hold, observe, and rethink the obvious. The first move may be simple, but the real solution is often hiding in the way the room behaves.

Discover 30 handcrafted levels in the first world, learn new puzzle rules without long tutorials, earn stars, unlock new stages, and use optional hints when you are truly stuck. The game is designed for quick sessions and satisfying “I should have seen that” moments.

Features:

- 30 handcrafted puzzle levels in the first world.
- Tap, drag, timing, physics, observation, and trick puzzles.
- Short sessions with deterministic solutions.
- Local progress saving with no account required.
- Optional rewarded hints and an optional remove-ads purchase.
- No chat, no weapons, and no adult content.

Looks easy. Think again.
