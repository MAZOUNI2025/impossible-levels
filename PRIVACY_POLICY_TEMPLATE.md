## Overview

IMPOSSIBLE LEVELS is a casual puzzle game for Android. The game is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. We do not intentionally collect names, contact lists, chat messages, precise location, or payment-card details inside the game.

## Data stored locally

The game stores gameplay progress, unlocked levels, stars, hint counts, coin balance, settings, and the remove-ads state on the device. This information is used to provide the game features and is not uploaded to the developer's server in the offline version.
